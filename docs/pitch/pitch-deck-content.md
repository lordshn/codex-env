# Workplane — AI moves fast. Your company stays in control.

12-slide investor pitch • Product concept • October 2026

Each slide has one message. The role/buyer segmentation and competitor comparison slides were removed. Detailed feature lists, market arithmetic, dollar pricing, funding allocations and appendices were removed.

## 01 — AI moves fast. Your company stays in control.

- One company platform for approved AI coding and agent work.
- Enable employees. Protect company data. Stay accountable.

## 02 — The tools arrived. The control didn’t.

- Data: Where does company context go?
- Agents: What can they access and change?
- Spending: What are we paying for—and getting back?

## 03 — When AI can act, policy must act too.

- Assistants suggest. People review the answer.
- Agents execute. They read context, call tools and run commands.
- Company rules need to work where the action happens.

## 04 — One place to make AI ready for company work.

- The company configures approved models, tools, context and rules.
- The employee launches a work environment that is ready to use.

## 05 — A ready-to-work AI environment. In one launch.

- Approved work profile: workspace, models, context and skills, tools and permissions.
- Publish once. Assign by role. Update centrally.
- Less setup for employees. Less integration work for IT.

## 06 — Keep company data on the approved path.

- Know what is being sent.
- Inspect before external dispatch.
- Use only approved services.
- Allow, redact, block or request review—under company policy.

## 07 — Give agents a job. And clear limits.

- Read the right repository. Run the permitted tools. Propose a change.
- Scope access. Require sensitive-action approval. Stop or revoke a run.

## 08 — Your models. Your infrastructure. Your choice.

- Local: company-operated services.
- Cloud: approved external providers.
- Hybrid: the right permitted path for the task.
- One company experience across qualified integrations.

## 09 — Know what AI costs. See what it delivers.

- Faster starts: from approved access to useful work.
- Accountable spend: usage, owners and run limits.
- Better decisions: measure review, rework and verified outcomes.

## 10 — A subscription to the platform. Freedom to choose the services.

- Proposed revenue model: annual platform subscription plus active employee usage.
- Customers bring their model providers and infrastructure.
- Land with one workflow. Expand as the company rolls out AI.

## 11 — Prove one workflow. Then make rollout repeatable.

- Pilot: one engineering group, one useful workflow.
- Validate: employee value, security and operating effort.
- Expand: more teams, more qualified capabilities.

## 12 — Make AI a company capability.

- The approved path from employee intent to governed AI work.
- Seeking pre-seed partners to turn this vision into a repeatable enterprise product.

## References and factual boundaries

The deck presents proposed capabilities, not implemented or measured results. The subscription model is a proposal; no customer, revenue, team-credential, market-size or funding-amount claims are made. The working name is not trademark-cleared. Controls apply to supported workflows and require qualification; no universal secret detection, complete bypass prevention or arbitrary integration compatibility is promised.

The timing slide links to Microsoft disclosures showing why agent permission boundaries matter. They do not establish exploitation or a customer breach.

- [Agent hook approval disclosure](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/security/advisories/GHSA-fq62-m6f5-c7h9) • Microsoft, October 13, 2026.
- [MCP shared permission disclosure](https://github.com/microsoft/vscode/security/advisories/GHSA-qghm-w43w-vx8v) • Microsoft, October 13, 2026.
